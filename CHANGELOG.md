# What's new

Newest first. Add `[critical]` after the version title to force everyone to update.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-26
- Customers can log in with Google, Facebook, their mobile number (text code) or email (code) — no password needed. New customers fill in their business details once.
- Staff and agents can sign up themselves in their app. They confirm their email with a 6-digit code and wait for the boss to approve.
- New "Sign-up requests" page for the boss: approve (pick the role) or reject.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-25
- First full build of the Sunny Foods app: Staff app, Customer ordering portal and Agent portal.
- Orders, packing checklist, deliveries, payments, stock with batches (oldest expiry first).
- Prices per customer, employee rates, vouchers, agent commissions and payouts.
- Master Control for access, Trash with restore, change history, 4 languages.
